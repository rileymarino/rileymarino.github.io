Student leadership · Cal Poly ASI

Getting a seat in a required course.

Course registration reform

As an elected director representing the Orfalea College of Business, I consulted campus staff and authored Resolution 26-09. I wanted the university to look beyond full classrooms and examine which missing courses could delay a student’s next semester.

The ASI Board adopted the resolution on May 27, 2026. It calls for analysis of waitlists, unmet seat demand and degree requirements, and asks the university to consider a joint task force with student representation.
