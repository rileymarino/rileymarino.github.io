AI Fashion App · Personal Project

LYFESTYLE

Would a different pair of shoes pull this outfit together?

I built LYFESTYLE with Gemini CLI to give a full-body outfit photo a playful score from 1 to 10. The app looks at how the colors, accessories, and individual pieces work together. A separate selfie tool suggests colors based on skin tone, while style guides and outfit templates offer ideas to try next.

It was one of my first AI projects and the one that got me hooked on building apps. It still runs on my phone, and my friends and I still pull it out to rate our outfits for fun.
